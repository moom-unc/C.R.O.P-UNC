Mission Statement

C.R.O.P seeks to make higher education approachable for rural and unsupported students.

Who We Serve & Our Target Audience

  • Underfunded rural high schools and students traditionally overlooked by standard academic programming.
  • First-generation college students and minority students seeking guidance on college opportunities.
  • High school students in standard classes who need encouragement to step up to honors or AP courses.
  • Students who lack a dedicated advocate or pathway to higher education opportunities.

Program Goals

We aim to provide tailored resources, mentorship, and continuous support to high school students on a personal level, encouraging them to pursue higher education confidence and clarity.

About Us

We are an organization at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill dedicated to providing direct resources, mentorship, and advising to rural and underserved high school students. Currently, we are connecting with host schools and expanding our team of dedicated UNC mentors!

UNC Campus Leadership & Executive Roles

Our general body meets bi-weekly, supported by specialized leadership teams including Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, Research Team, Outreach Chair, Co-Communication Chairs, and Lead Mentors.

Our 3 Pillars

1. Sprout (Freshmen & Sophomores)

Focuses on long-term high school class planning, career pathways, exploring RCC/AP classes, personality assessments, and summer opportunity planning.

2. Grow (Juniors)

Focuses on researching colleges and majors, maximizing AP/RCC course transferability, summer program opportunities, and preparing for senior year admissions.

3. Flourish (Seniors)

Focuses on finding scholarships, completing regular action college applications (target: 3+ applications), essay reviews, resume workshops, and elevator pitches.

How Our Mentorship Works

High school members meet monthly with a faculty liaison, while being paired with a dedicated UNC Mentor (target 3:1 student-to-mentor ratio). Mentors and mentees complete monthly interactive "missions" and attend campus visits, professional career panels, and volunteer events together.
